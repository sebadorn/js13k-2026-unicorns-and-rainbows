# js13k 2026: Rainbow in the Dark

![Screenshot from the game.](cover_800x500.png?raw=true)

Entry for the [js13k competition of 2026](https://2026.js13kgames.com/).  
Theme: **Unicorns and Rainbows**


## Description

When the Darkness swallowed everything, it seemed like an end.  
But some motes of magic still stirred.  
Colorful unicorn magic, painting on a dark canvas.

Select a color to draw towers and fighters to defend against incoming waves of enemies.

Colors have different effects:
- For towers they are applied in a radius in a regular interval
- For fighters they are applied as a boost to their base stats
- For tower projectiles and fighter weapons they trigger on attack

For what the colors actually do: Play around to find out!


## Controls

Use your mouse to click and draw.

`[Esc]` to pause and unpause.
